Delete Your myMinto Account

myMinto, operated by Frillings · Last updated: 10 July 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your myMinto account and the personal data associated with it.

Option 1 — Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open the myMinto app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm, then type DELETE when prompted.
Your account is deleted immediately.

Option 2 — Request by email

If you cannot access the app, email customer_care@myminto.in from the email address on your account and ask us to delete it. We may verify your identity before processing the request.

What is deleted

  • Your profile — name, phone number, and email address
  • Your saved delivery addresses
  • Your sign-in identity (so the email/phone can be reused)
  • Your device notification tokens

What is kept

  • Past orders are retained for the shops' and platform's accounting records, but are anonymised — no longer linked to you or your personal details.
  • Limited records may be retained where required by law, tax, or fraud-prevention obligations, and are deleted once those obligations expire.
