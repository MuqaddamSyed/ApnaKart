1. Information we collect

DataWhy we collect it

2. How we use your information

To create and manage your account, place and deliver orders, show nearby shops, send order updates, provide customer support, and keep the service secure. We do not sell your personal data.

3. Who we share it with

We share only what is necessary to fulfil your order:

  • Shops (suppliers) and delivery agents involved in your order receive your delivery address, location pin, and phone number.
  • Service providers that operate our infrastructure: Supabase (database & hosting), Brevo (email OTP), Firebase Cloud Messaging (notifications), and MapTiler / Google Maps (maps & navigation).
  • Legal: where required by law.

4. Data retention

We keep your personal data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, your personal data is removed and past orders are anonymised (kept for the shops' accounting records but no longer linked to you).

5. Deleting your account

You can delete your account and personal data at any time, directly in the app: Profile → Delete account. This permanently removes your profile, saved addresses, and sign-in identity. To request deletion by other means, contact us at [SUPPORT EMAIL].

6. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access to the database is restricted by row-level security so users can only access their own data.

7. Children

myMinto is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

8. Your rights

You may access, correct, or delete your data. Account and address data can be edited in the app; for other requests contact us at [SUPPORT EMAIL].

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
