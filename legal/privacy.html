Privacy Policy — myMinto

Last Updated: 10 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Frillings ("myMinto", "we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects your personal information when you use the myMinto mobile application, website, and related services.

By accessing or using myMinto, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide and improve our services.

InformationPurpose
Full NameTo identify your account and orders
Mobile NumberAccount verification, order updates, customer support, and communication with shops and delivery partners
Email AddressLogin authentication, OTP verification, account notifications, and support
Delivery AddressDeliver orders accurately and save frequently used addresses
GPS Location (Approximate or Precise)To show nearby shops, estimate delivery time, calculate delivery charges, and assist delivery partners. Location is accessed only while the app is in use unless you explicitly grant additional permissions.
Order HistoryTo display previous orders, customer support, dispute resolution, and improve our services
Device InformationDevice type, operating system, app version, IP address, crash reports, and diagnostics for security and performance
Push Notification TokenTo send order status updates, promotional notifications (where permitted), and service announcements
Customer Support MessagesTo investigate complaints, improve service quality, and resolve disputes

Important: myMinto does not collect or store debit card, credit card, UPI PINs, banking passwords, or other payment credentials. Orders are currently fulfilled using Cash on Delivery (COD).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Authenticate your identity.
  • Process and deliver orders.
  • Connect you with nearby shops and delivery partners.
  • Send order confirmations and delivery updates.
  • Respond to customer support requests.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access.
  • Improve application performance and user experience.
  • Comply with legal obligations.
  • Send promotional offers and updates where permitted by law, with an option to opt out where applicable.

We do not sell your personal information to advertisers or third parties.

3. Location Information

Location access helps us:

  • Display shops near you.
  • Estimate delivery time.
  • Calculate delivery charges.
  • Guide delivery partners to your location.

We access your location only when necessary to provide these services and according to the permissions you grant on your device.

4. Information Sharing

We share your information only when necessary.

Shops

The shop fulfilling your order receives: your name, delivery address, contact number, ordered products, and delivery instructions (if provided).

Delivery Partners

Delivery partners receive: your name, contact number, delivery address, location pin, and delivery instructions.

Technology Service Providers

We may share limited information with trusted service providers that help operate our platform, including:

  • Supabase — Database, authentication, and backend infrastructure
  • Brevo — Email delivery and OTP verification
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) — Push notifications
  • MapTiler and/or Google Maps — Maps, routing, and location services

These providers process data only as necessary to provide their services.

Legal Requirements

We may disclose personal information when required by applicable law, court orders, government authorities, law enforcement agencies, or regulatory bodies, or when necessary to protect our legal rights, customers, delivery partners, or the public.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to operate your account, fulfil orders, resolve disputes, and meet legal, accounting, and regulatory obligations.

When you permanently delete your account:

  • Your personal profile is removed.
  • Saved addresses are deleted.
  • Authentication credentials are removed.
  • Order history is anonymized where reasonably possible.
  • Certain records may be retained if required by law, tax regulations, fraud prevention, or dispute resolution.

6. Account Deletion

You may permanently delete your account at any time through Profile → Delete Account. Deleting your account permanently removes your personal profile and associated information, subject to any legal retention requirements.

You may also request deletion by contacting customer_care@myminto.in. Identity verification may be required before processing deletion requests.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including:

  • HTTPS encryption for data transmission.
  • Secure authentication.
  • Database access controls.
  • Row-Level Security (RLS) where applicable.
  • Limited employee access based on business need.
  • Continuous monitoring for unauthorized access.

While we strive to protect your information, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission is completely secure. Therefore, absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Access your personal information.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Update your account information.
  • Delete your account.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Request information about how your personal data is processed.

Most account information can be managed directly within the app. For additional requests, contact customer_care@myminto.in.

9. Children's Privacy

myMinto is intended for individuals aged 13 years or older. If you are under 18, you should use the service with the guidance or permission of a parent or legal guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If we become aware that personal information has been collected from a child under 13 without appropriate consent, we will take reasonable steps to delete that information.

10. Third-Party Services

Our application may use services provided by third parties. These services maintain their own privacy policies and practices. Examples include Supabase, Firebase Cloud Messaging, Google Maps, MapTiler, and Brevo. We encourage users to review the privacy policies of these providers where applicable.

11. Cookies and Analytics

Where applicable, our website or application may use cookies, local storage, and analytics technologies to remember user preferences, improve performance, understand usage patterns, and diagnose technical issues. You may disable cookies through your browser settings, although some features may not function correctly.

12. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any significant changes will be reflected by updating the Last Updated date at the top of this page. Continued use of myMinto after changes become effective constitutes acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.
