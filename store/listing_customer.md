## Full description (Play ≤4000 / App Store ≤4000)

myMinto brings your neighbourhood to your doorstep. Order groceries, fresh food,
household essentials and more from local shops around you — and get them
delivered in minutes.

Built for your town, myMinto connects you directly with trusted local shops and
delivery partners. No long waits, no card required — just simple, fast delivery
you can rely on.

WHY myMinto

• Shop local, delivered fast — order from shops near you in just a few taps
• Live order tracking — watch your delivery on the map and see your ETA
• Cash on Delivery — pay when your order arrives, no card or UPI PIN needed
• Multiple shops, one order — add items from different shops in a single go
• Real-time updates — know exactly when your order is confirmed, packed and on its way
• Save your addresses — set your home or work once and reorder in seconds

WHAT YOU CAN ORDER

• Groceries and daily essentials
• Fresh fruits and vegetables
• Hot food from local restaurants
• Household and personal-care items
• And more from shops in your area

HOW IT WORKS

1. Sign in with your email — quick and secure
2. Set your delivery location
3. Browse nearby shops and add items to your cart
4. Place your order and pay Cash on Delivery
5. Track your delivery live until it reaches your door

myMinto is made for Tier 2 and Tier 3 towns — fast, simple, and reliable, with
Cash on Delivery so anyone can order without a bank card.

Download myMinto and get your essentials delivered in minutes.
